What to say in Japanese

1. Say what happened

Pick the phrase that fits and say it.

  • 火事ですKaji desuThere is a fire
  • 救急ですKyūkyū desuI need an ambulance
  • 事件ですJiken desuA crime has happened
  • 事故ですJiko desuThere has been an accident

2. Say where you are

  • 住所は〇〇ですJūsho wa ○○ desuThe address is ...

3. If Japanese is hard

  • 日本語があまり話せませんNihongo ga amari hanasemasenI don't speak much Japanese
