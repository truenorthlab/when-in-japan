Emergencies & disasters

Good to know

If someone collapses, call for help and ask people to dial 119 and bring an AED

If you see someone suddenly collapse or not respond, first check that the area is safe, then shout for help, for example "Someone come! A person has collapsed!" When people come, give each a clear task, such as "Please call 119" and "Please bring an AED". Even if you do not know what to do, the 119 dispatcher on the phone will tell you how to help. Putting the phone on speaker keeps both hands free. AEDs are placed in stations, airports, public facilities and elsewhere, and once opened, they guide you with voice messages and lights.

Machine translation

Do

  • First check that the area is safe
  • Shout for help, and choose people to call 119 and bring an AED
  • On a 119 call, stay calm, answer the questions and follow the instructions
  • Use an AED by following its voice messages and lights

Avoid

  • Walk away and do nothing because you do not know what to do

Original Japanese

倒れている人を見たら、人を呼んで119番とAEDを頼む

突然倒れた人や反応のない人を見たら、まず周りの安全を確かめてから、大きな声で「誰か来て！人が倒れています！」と助けを求めます。来た人には「あなたは119番へ通報してください」「あなたはAEDを持ってきてください」と具体的に頼みます。やり方が分からなくても、119番の電話で通信指令員が手当ての方法を教えてくれます。電話はスピーカーにすると両手が使えます。AEDは駅や空港、公共施設などに置かれていて、ふたを開けると音声とランプで操作を教えてくれます。
