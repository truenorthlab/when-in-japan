Don't eat while walking in crowded places

Eating while walking is seen as impolite in most public places. In narrow or crowded streets, you may also soil other people's clothes.

Machine translation

Do

  • Eat where you can sit or stand still
  • Carry your trash and dispose of it later

Avoid

  • Eat while walking along a crowded street

混雑した場所では歩きながら食べない

歩きながら食べるのは、多くの場所で行儀が悪いと受け取られます。狭い道や混雑した場所では、ほかの人の服を汚すこともあります。
