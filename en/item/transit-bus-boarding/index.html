Trains & buses

Good to know

How you pay on a bus depends on the route

On buses where the fare depends on distance, take a numbered ticket when you board, then check the number on the board and pay when you get off. On buses with a flat fare, you pay when you board. Some buses do not accept IC cards, and some machines do not give change.

Machine translation

Do

  • Take a numbered ticket when you board, if there is one
  • Check whether IC cards work and have coins ready

Avoid

  • Board without taking the ticket

Original Japanese

バスの運賃の払い方は路線で違う

距離で運賃が変わるバスでは、乗るときに番号の書かれた整理券を受け取り、降りるときにボードで番号を確かめて運賃を払います。どこまで乗っても同じ運賃のバスでは、乗るときに払います。IC カードが使えないバスや、おつりが出ない機械もあります。
