Legal rule

Drive on the left, and always stop at red lights and stop signs

In Japan, vehicles drive on the left side of the road. Stop at red lights, and where there is a stop sign (止まれ), come to a full stop before the stop line and check left and right. When someone is crossing or about to cross at a crosswalk, stop before it and let them go first. Traffic rules and signs in Japan can differ from those in other countries.

Machine translation

Do

  • Drive on the left side of the road
  • Come to a full stop at stop signs and check left and right
  • Give way to pedestrians at crosswalks

Avoid

  • Go through a red light
  • Drive through a crosswalk without stopping when someone is about to cross

  • Never drive after drinkingThe Road Traffic Act prohibits driving under the influence of alcohol, regardless of the amount. Not only the driver but also those who lend the car, serve the alcohol or ride along can be punished.Legal rule
  • Follow the signals and use crosswalksPedestrians must also obey traffic signals. If there is a crosswalk nearby, cross there. Do not cross the road diagonally except where signs allow it, and do not cross right in front of or right behind a vehicle. Standing in the road in a way that blocks traffic, such as stopping in the roadway to take photos, is also prohibited by law.Legal rule
Original Japanese

車は左側通行。赤信号と「止まれ」で必ず止まる

日本では、車は道路の中央から左の部分を通行します。赤信号では止まり、「止まれ」の標識がある場所では停止線の手前で一時停止して、左右の安全を確かめます。横断歩道を渡っている人や渡ろうとしている人がいるときは、手前で一時停止して道を譲ります。日本の交通ルールや標識は、ほかの国と違うことがあります。
