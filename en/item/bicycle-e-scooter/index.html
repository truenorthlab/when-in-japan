Bicycles

Law

Check the traffic rules before riding an electric kick scooter

Most electric kick scooters are classed as "specified small motorized bicycles" (特定小型原動機付自転車), with rules set under the Road Traffic Act. People under 16 may not ride them. As a rule, ride on the left side of the road. You may ride on a sidewalk only where a sign allows it, after switching to the set speed mode with the maximum speed lamp flashing. Obey traffic lights, never ride after drinking, and do not ride two people. A scooter you own needs compulsory automobile liability insurance and a license plate. Riding a scooter that does not meet the standards requires a driver's license, and you may not ride it on sidewalks.

Related items

  • Drive on the left, and always stop at red lights and stop signsIn Japan, vehicles drive on the left side of the road. Stop at red lights, and where there is a stop sign (止まれ), come to a full stop before the stop line and check left and right. When someone is crossing or about to cross at a crosswalk, stop before it and let them go first. Traffic rules and signs in Japan can differ from those in other countries.Law
  • Never drive after drinkingThe Road Traffic Act prohibits driving under the influence of alcohol, regardless of the amount. Not only the driver but also those who lend the car, serve the alcohol or ride along can be punished.Law
Original Japanese

電動キックボードは交通ルールを確かめてから乗る

電動キックボードの多くは「特定小型原動機付自転車」として、道路交通法のルールが決まっています。16歳未満は運転できません。原則として車道の左側を走り、歩道を走れるのは、決められた速度に切り替えて最高速度表示灯を点滅させ、通行できる表示がある歩道だけです。信号を守り、お酒を飲んだら乗らず、二人乗りはしません。自分で持つ車体には、自賠責保険とナンバープレートが必要です。基準に合わない車体を運転するには運転免許が必要で、歩道は走れません。
