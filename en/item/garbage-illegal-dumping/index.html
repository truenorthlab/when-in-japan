Garbage & recycling

Law

Do not litter or dump garbage illegally

The Waste Management Act says no one may improperly discard waste, and violations carry penalties. Leaving garbage on a road or in the mountains is one example. Burning garbage outdoors, such as in a yard or an empty lot (野焼き), is also prohibited except in cases the law allows, and carries penalties. Carelessly throwing cans, cigarette butts and other litter on streets or in parks is also prohibited by the Minor Offenses Act (軽犯罪法).

Machine translation

Do

  • Dispose of garbage the way your municipality sets out
  • If you don't know how, ask your municipality

Avoid

  • Dump garbage on roads, in the mountains or similar places
  • Throw cans or cigarette butts on the street
  • Burn garbage in a yard or an empty lot

Official information

Original Japanese

道や山にごみを捨てない（ポイ捨て・不法投棄）

廃棄物処理法は、誰であっても、みだりに廃棄物を捨ててはならないと定めており、違反には罰則があります。道路や山にごみを捨てることなどがこれにあたります。ごみを庭や空き地などで燃やすこと（野焼き）も、法律が定める例外を除いて禁じられており、罰則があります。空き缶や吸い殻などを道や公園にみだりに捨てることは、軽犯罪法でも禁じられています。
