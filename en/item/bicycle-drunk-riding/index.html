Bicycles

Legal rule

Don't ride a bicycle after drinking

The Road Traffic Act says no one may drive a vehicle under the influence of alcohol, and this includes bicycles. Not only the rider but also those who provide the bicycle or alcohol, and those riding along, can be punished.

Machine translation

Do

  • After drinking, go home by public transport or on foot

Avoid

  • Ride because you only had a little
  • Serve alcohol to someone who is about to ride

Official information

Original Japanese

お酒を飲んだら自転車に乗らない

道路交通法は「何人も、酒気を帯びて車両等を運転してはならない」と定めており、自転車も含まれます。運転した人だけでなく、自転車やお酒を提供した人、同乗した人も処罰の対象になります。
