Law

Take tax-free purchases out of Japan after customs confirmation at departure

When you shop at a tax-free store, you pay the price including tax. When you leave Japan, you show your passport at a terminal at the airport or seaport to receive customs confirmation, and then the consumption tax is refunded. Get the confirmation before checking in your luggage. If even one item on a receipt is missing, nothing on that receipt is refunded. Consumables eaten or used in Japan are not refunded either. If you do not take items confirmed by customs out of Japan, the consumption tax is collected and you may also face penalties. Check the deadlines and refund methods on the official page or with the store.

Machine translation

Do

  • On your departure day, get customs confirmation before checking in your luggage
  • Leave Japan carrying all the items on each receipt together

Avoid

  • Sell or give away in Japan items that have received customs confirmation
  • Fail to take confirmed items out of Japan

Original Japanese

免税で買った物は、出国時に税関の確認を受けて国外に持ち出す

免税店での買い物は、税込みの値段で払い、出国するときに空港や港の端末でパスポートを見せて税関の確認を受けると、消費税分が返金される仕組みです。確認は、荷物を預ける前に受けます。1枚の領収書の物を1つでも持っていないと、その領収書の分は返金されません。日本で食べたり使ったりした消耗品も返金されません。税関の確認を受けた物を国外に持ち出さないと、消費税を取られ、罰則の対象にもなります。期限や返金の方法は、公式ページやお店で確かめます。
