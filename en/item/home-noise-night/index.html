Home & neighbors

Good to know

Keep noise down from night to early morning

In apartment buildings, sound travels easily to the rooms next door, above and below. Take particular care from night until early morning. With musical instruments and audio equipment too, mind the volume and the time of day.

Machine translation

Do

  • Keep voices and TV volume low
  • Mind the noise of vacuum cleaners, washing machines and doors
  • Mind the volume and time of day when playing instruments or using speakers

Avoid

  • Hold parties late at night
  • Make loud noise bathing late at night

Original Japanese

夜から早朝は生活音に気をつける

集合住宅では、隣や上下の部屋に音が伝わりやすいです。特に夜間から早朝にかけては注意しましょう。楽器やオーディオの音も、音量と時間帯に気をつけましょう。
