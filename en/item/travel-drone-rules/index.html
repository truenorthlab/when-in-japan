Legal rule

Check the flight rules before flying a drone

Flying a drone in Japan may require registering the aircraft. Without permission, you cannot fly around airports, over densely populated districts and in similar areas.

Machine translation

Do

  • Check registration and where you may fly in the official guidance first
  • Also check the rules of shrines, temples, parks and other facilities

Avoid

  • Fly without permission around airports or over city areas

Original Japanese

ドローンは飛行ルールを確認してから飛ばす

日本でドローンを飛ばすには、機体の登録が必要な場合があります。空港の周辺や人が多い地区などでは、許可なく飛ばせません。
