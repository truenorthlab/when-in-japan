Good to know

Be quiet and respectful at shrines and temples

Shrines and temples are places of worship. Behave quietly and follow the signs and instructions. If you are unsure how to pray, simply following those around you is fine.

Machine translation

Do

  • Behave quietly
  • Take off your shoes where instructed
  • Follow no-photography signs

Avoid

  • Enter restricted areas
  • Visit in very revealing clothing
  • Write or scratch on buildings and cultural assets
