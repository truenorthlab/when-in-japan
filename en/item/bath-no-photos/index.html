Public baths & hot springs

Legal rule

No photos in changing rooms or baths

Taking photos in changing rooms and bathing areas is prohibited by facility rules. In addition, secretly photographing someone's naked body or other sexual images can be punished by law.

Machine translation

Do

Avoid

  • Secretly photograph anyone's naked body
