Law

Don't take shellfish, sea cucumbers and other sea life without permission (poaching)

Taking abalone or sea cucumbers without a license or similar authorization is punished, whether or not you are a fisher and regardless of how much you took or where. Taking turban shells, spiny lobsters and other species covered by fishing rights without authorization is also poaching. This is the same even for things you find on the beach or rocky shore. Fishing and clam digging also have rules set by law and by each prefecture, covering the gear you may use, closed areas and seasons, size limits and more. Recreational fishing by people without Japanese nationality is allowed only within a limited range, so check with the prefecture or the fishing boat operator beforehand.

Related items

  • At the sea or a river, do not swim where it is prohibited, and wear a life jacketAt the sea, swim at beaches with lifeguards or monitors, inside the designated swimming area. Never swim where swimming is prohibited. Check the wind direction and wave height, and do not enter the water near river mouths, along breakwaters or around rocks, where strong currents flowing out to sea (rip currents) tend to form. Rivers can look calm but have uneven currents, and the water can rise suddenly when it rains upstream. Near water, wear a life jacket, keep your eyes on children, and do not play in the water when you feel unwell.Good to know
  • Don't catch wild birds or animalsWild birds and mammals are, as a rule, protected under the Wildlife Protection and Management Act, and catching or harming them without permission is prohibited. Taking bird eggs is also prohibited. This includes familiar birds in towns and parks, such as pigeons, sparrows and crows. Removing a nest with eggs or chicks in it is also prohibited. Do not catch them to eat or keep as pets; just watch them.Law
Original Japanese

海の貝やナマコなどを勝手に採らない（密漁）

アワビとナマコは、許可などなしに採ると、漁業者かどうか、採った量や場所にかかわらず処罰されます。サザエやイセエビなど漁業権の対象になっている生き物を、権限なく採ることも密漁です。浜辺や磯で見つけたものでも同じです。釣りや潮干狩りにも法律や都道府県のルールがあり、使える道具や禁止の区域・時期、大きさの制限などが決められています。日本の国籍がない人の釣りなどは認められる範囲が限られているため、事前に都道府県や釣り船の業者に確かめます。
