Watch out for heatstroke on hot days

Heatstroke can happen indoors and at night, and it can be life-threatening. When a heatstroke alert is issued, drink water and take salt before you feel thirsty, use your air conditioner properly, and avoid going out unless necessary. People around older people and others at risk should check on them.

Machine translation

Do

  • Drink water and take salt before you feel thirsty
  • Use the air conditioner instead of putting up with the heat
  • Check the heatstroke alerts

Avoid

  • Push yourself to exercise outdoors on very hot days
