Restaurants & convenience stores

Manners

Don't stand chopsticks in rice or pass food chopstick to chopstick

Sticking chopsticks upright in rice (tatebashi) and passing food from chopsticks to chopsticks (hashiwatashi) are avoided because they resemble funeral customs.

Machine translation

Do

  • When sharing food, place it on a small plate and pass that

Avoid

  • Stand chopsticks upright in rice
  • Pass food from chopsticks to chopsticks

Official information

Original Japanese

箸を立てない・箸から箸へ渡さない

ご飯に箸を突き刺して立てる「立て箸」と、箸と箸で料理を受け渡す「箸渡し」は、葬儀の作法と同じ動作なので避けられています。
