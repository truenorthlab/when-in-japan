Local or facility rule

Follow ski resort rules and don't casually leave the managed area

Outside a ski resort's managed area is winter mountain terrain, completely different from groomed slopes. There are hard-to-predict dangers such as sudden weather changes, avalanches, getting lost and hitting trees. Because there are no course markers or ropes, you may enter without noticing. If you call for rescue, you may be charged large search costs, and rescue may be impossible in bad weather. Follow the resort's rules and stay out of closed areas.

Related items

  • On designated mountain trails, submit a climbing plan before you goBy ordinance, Nagano Prefecture designates trails on mountains with a high risk of accidents as "designated mountain trails" and requires a climbing plan to be submitted before using them. Gifu Prefecture also has mountains where submitting a climbing notice is mandatory. Submitting online is recommended, and you can also send it by post or drop it in the box at the trailhead. Some trailheads do not have a box. On the day of the climb, carry the plan you made with you.Local or facility ruleApplies in: Designated mountain trails in Nagano, Gifu and other prefectures
  • Be prepared so you don't run into bears in the mountainsIn mountains and forests, you may come across bears. Let them know people are around with a bell or your voice, don't go alone, and don't leave food, bags or rubbish behind. If you meet a bear, don't approach it. Stay calm, back away slowly while watching it, and don't turn your back and run.Good to know
Original Japanese

スキー場のルールを守り、管理区域の外に気軽に出ない

スキー場の管理区域の外は、整備されたゲレンデとはまったく違う冬山です。天気の急変や雪崩、道迷い、木への衝突など、予測しにくい危険があります。コースの表示やロープなどの目印がないため、気づかないうちに入ってしまうこともあります。救助を頼むと多額の捜索費用を請求されることがあり、天気が悪いと救助できないこともあります。スキー場のルールを守り、立入禁止の場所には入りません。
