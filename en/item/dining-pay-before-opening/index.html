Restaurants & convenience stores

Manners

Do not open products before you pay

In Japanese stores, you pay at the register before you open a product. Even if you plan to pay later, opening, eating or drinking something before checkout is seen as bad manners in Japan. It may be no problem in some countries, but people in Japan take it especially seriously.

Machine translation

Do

  • Pay at the register before you open anything

Avoid

  • Open products before checkout
  • Eat or drink in the store before you pay

Official information

Original Japanese

会計の前に商品を開けない

日本の店では、商品はレジで代金を払ってから開けます。あとで払うつもりでも、会計前に開けたり食べたり飲んだりすることは、日本ではマナー違反と受け止められます。国によっては気にされないことでも、日本では特に厳しく見られる行動です。
