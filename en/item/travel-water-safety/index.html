Travel & sightseeing

Good to know

At the sea or a river, do not swim where it is prohibited, and wear a life jacket

At the sea, swim at beaches with lifeguards or monitors, inside the designated swimming area. Never swim where swimming is prohibited. Check the wind direction and wave height, and do not enter the water near river mouths, along breakwaters or around rocks, where strong currents flowing out to sea (rip currents) tend to form. Rivers can look calm but have uneven currents, and the water can rise suddenly when it rains upstream. Near water, wear a life jacket, keep your eyes on children, and do not play in the water when you feel unwell.

Machine translation

Do

  • Swim in the designated area of a beach with lifeguards
  • Wear a life jacket near water
  • Keep your eyes on children and stay within arm's reach

Avoid

  • Swim where swimming is prohibited
  • Enter the water near river mouths, breakwaters or rocks
  • Go near a river in bad weather or when it is raining upstream

Original Japanese

海や川では、遊泳禁止の場所で泳がず、ライフジャケットを着る

海では、ライフセーバーや監視員がいる海水浴場で、決められた遊泳エリアの中で泳ぎます。遊泳禁止の場所では絶対に泳ぎません。風の向きや波の高さを確かめ、沖へ向かう強い流れ（離岸流）が起きやすい河口付近、堤防沿い、岩場には入りません。川は穏やかに見えても流れが一定でないことがあり、上流で雨が降ると一気に水位が上がることがあります。水辺ではライフジャケットを着て、子どもから目を離さず、体調が悪いときは遊びません。
