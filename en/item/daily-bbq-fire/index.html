Local or facility rule

Check whether barbecues are allowed before having one by a river or in a park

Whether you can barbecue on a riverbank or in a park is decided place by place. Some areas do not allow barbecues, and some places prohibit the use of fire. Lighting a fire directly on the ground is prohibited in many places. Before you start, check the guidance from the river office, the local government or the facility manager. On riverbanks, also watch out for sudden rises in the river.

  • Do not litter or dump garbage illegallyThe Waste Management Act says no one may improperly discard waste, and violations carry penalties. Leaving garbage on a road or in the mountains is one example. Burning garbage outdoors, such as in a yard or an empty lot (野焼き), is also prohibited except in cases the law allows, and carries penalties. Carelessly throwing cans, cigarette butts and other litter on streets or in parks is also prohibited by the Minor Offenses Act (軽犯罪法).Law
  • At the sea or a river, do not swim where it is prohibited, and wear a life jacketAt the sea, swim at beaches with lifeguards or monitors, inside the designated swimming area. Never swim where swimming is prohibited. Check the wind direction and wave height, and do not enter the water near river mouths, along breakwaters or around rocks, where strong currents flowing out to sea (rip currents) tend to form. Rivers can look calm but have uneven currents, and the water can rise suddenly when it rains upstream. Near water, wear a life jacket, keep your eyes on children, and do not play in the water when you feel unwell.Good to know
