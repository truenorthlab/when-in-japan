Travel & sightseeing

Manners

Be considerate of people and places in photos

Ask before photographing people you don't know. Don't enter private land or go past fences for a photo, and follow no-photography signs.

Machine translation

Do

  • Ask permission before photographing people
  • Follow no-photography signs and staff instructions
  • Stay on marked paths

Avoid

  • Enter private land or go past fences for a photo
  • Step into the road to take a photo
  • Take photos in dangerous places such as near railway tracks
