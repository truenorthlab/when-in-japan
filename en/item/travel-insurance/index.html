Good to know

Get travel insurance, and always pay your hospital bills

Travelers who are not covered by Japan's public health insurance may have to pay the full cost of hospital treatment themselves. Before your trip, take out travel insurance with sufficient coverage. Some travel insurance can be purchased even after arriving in Japan. If you leave medical bills unpaid, you may face stricter immigration screening the next time you come to Japan, and may even be refused entry.

Original Japanese

旅行保険に入り、病院の費用は必ず払う

日本の公的医療保険に入っていない旅行者は、病院の費用が全額自己負担になることがあります。旅行の前に、十分な補償のある旅行保険に入ります。日本に来てから入れる旅行保険もあります。医療費を払わないままにすると、次に日本に来るときの入国審査が厳しくなり、入国を断られることもあります。
