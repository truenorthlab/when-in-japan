Garbage & recycling

Legal rule

Recycle air conditioners, TVs, fridges and washers

Air conditioners, TVs, refrigerators and freezers, and washing machines and clothes dryers fall under the Home Appliance Recycling Act. Hand them to an appliance retailer or follow your municipality's instructions. The consumer pays the recycling and collection fees.

Machine translation

Do

  • Ask an appliance retailer or check your municipality's instructions
  • Check the recycling and collection fees

Avoid

  • Hand them to an unlicensed collector

Official information

Original Japanese

エアコン・テレビ・冷蔵庫・洗濯機はリサイクルに出す

エアコン、テレビ、冷蔵庫・冷凍庫、洗濯機・衣類乾燥機は家電リサイクル法の対象です。家電小売店に引き渡すか、市区町村の案内する方法でリサイクルします。リサイクル料金と収集運搬料金は消費者が負担します。
