Crime & the law

Legal rule

Do not keep what you find

If you find something someone has lost, return it to the owner or hand it in to the police. If you find it inside a station, shop or similar facility, give it to the staff. Keeping it may lead to a charge of embezzling lost property.

Machine translation

Do

  • On the street, return it to the owner or take it to a police station or koban
  • In a station, shop or hotel, hand it to the staff right away

Avoid

  • Keep a wallet or money you found

Official information

Original Japanese

拾った物を自分のものにしない

落とし物を拾ったら、落とし主に返すか警察に届けます。駅や店などの施設の中で拾ったときは、係員や店員に届けます。自分のものにすると、遺失物横領の罪に問われる可能性があります。
