Manners

Stay behind the yellow line on platforms

Near the edge of a platform you can be hit by a train or fall onto the tracks. Walk and wait on the inner side of the yellow tactile paving, which is there for people with visual impairments.

Machine translation

Do

  • Walk and wait behind the yellow line

Avoid

  • Walk along the edge of the platform
  • Lean on the platform doors

Original Japanese

ホームでは黄色い線の内側を歩く

駅のホームの端は、電車と接触したり線路に落ちたりするおそれがあります。目の不自由な人のための黄色い点字ブロックより内側を歩き、内側で電車を待ちます。
