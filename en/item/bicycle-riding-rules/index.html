Bicycles

Legal rule

Don't ride a bicycle holding an umbrella, with a passenger or side by side

Riding a bicycle while holding an umbrella, riding with earphones so that you can't hear the sounds around you, carrying a passenger, riding side by side, and riding without a light at night are prohibited by law or prefectural rules, and are subject to fines under the “blue ticket” system. There are allowed exceptions, such as carrying a small child in a child seat.

Machine translation

Do

  • Wear rain gear on rainy days
  • Turn on your light at night
  • Ride in single file

Avoid

  • Ride while holding an umbrella
  • Use earphones so that you can't hear what is around you
  • Carry a passenger
  • Ride side by side
