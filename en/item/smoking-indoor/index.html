Smoking & drinking

Legal rule

Indoor spaces are smoke-free in principle

Under the Health Promotion Act, indoor areas of facilities used by many people, including restaurants, are smoke-free in principle. Smoking is allowed in smoking rooms that meet the requirements. Schools, hospitals, child welfare facilities and government offices are in principle smoke-free across the whole site. People under 20 may not enter smoking areas.

Machine translation

Do

  • Look for signs for smoking rooms and smoking areas

屋内は原則禁煙

健康増進法により、多くの人が利用する施設や飲食店などの屋内は原則禁煙です。要件を満たす喫煙室では吸えます。学校、病院、児童福祉施設、行政機関などは原則として敷地内禁煙です。20歳未満の人は喫煙エリアに入れません。
