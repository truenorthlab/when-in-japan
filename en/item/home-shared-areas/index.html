Home & neighbors

Local or facility rule

Keep hallways and stairs clear

Hallways and stairs outside your room are shared areas and evacuation routes in an emergency. A balcony may also be an evacuation route.

Machine translation

Do

  • Keep belongings and garbage inside your own room
  • Keep the area around a balcony escape hatch clear

Avoid

  • Leave belongings or garbage in hallways or on stairs

廊下や階段に物を置かない

自分の部屋以外の廊下や階段は共用部分で、いざというときの避難通路です。ベランダが避難通路になっている場合もあります。
