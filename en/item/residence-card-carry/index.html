Residence status

Law

Always carry your residence card

Mid- to long-term residents must always carry their residence card and show it when asked. Those under 16 are exempt from carrying it. Special permanent residents need not always carry their certificate but must show it when asked. Short-term visitors without a residence card carry their passport.

Machine translation

Do

  • Take your residence card whenever you go out
  • Show it when asked by a police officer or other official

Avoid

  • Leave your residence card behind because you have your passport

在留カードは常に持ち歩く

中長期在留者は在留カードを常に携帯する義務があり、求められたら提示します。16歳未満は携帯が免除されます。特別永住者は証明書を常に持ち歩く必要はありませんが、求められたら提示します。在留カードのない短期滞在の人はパスポートを携帯します。
