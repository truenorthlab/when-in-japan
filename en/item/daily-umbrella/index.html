Good to know

Hold your umbrella so it does not hit people

On rainy days, a wet umbrella that touches people or a tip pointed at the person behind you is a nuisance and can be dangerous. Hold a closed umbrella close to your body with the tip pointing down.

Machine translation

Do

  • Hold a closed umbrella close to your body with the tip down

Avoid

  • Carry an umbrella sideways
  • Let a wet umbrella touch people or seats

Official information

Original Japanese

傘は人に当たらないように持つ

雨の日に、ぬれた傘が人に当たったり、傘の先を後ろの人に向けたりすると、迷惑で危険です。たたんだ傘は先を下に向けて、体の近くで持ちます。
