Trains & buses

Manners

Let people off first, then board in line

When boarding a train, passengers getting off go first. Wait in line on the platform and board in the order you lined up.

Machine translation

Do

  • Line up to the side of the doors
  • Board after passengers have gotten off

Avoid

  • Cut in line
  • Try to board before others get off

Show in Japanese

降りる人を待ってから、並んだ順に乗る

電車に乗るときは、降りる人が先です。ホームでは列に並んで待ち、並んだ順番を守って乗ります。
