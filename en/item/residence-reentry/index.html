Legal rule

Check re-entry rules before leaving Japan

People with a valid passport and residence card can come back without a re-entry permit within a set period if they say they intend to return when leaving (special re-entry). This period can't be extended abroad. If you expect to go beyond it, get a re-entry permit before you leave. Check the period on the official page.

Machine translation

Do

  • Before leaving, check your period of stay and your return plans
  • When leaving, tell the immigration officer you intend to return

Avoid

  • Stay abroad beyond the period without a permit

Original Japanese

日本を出て戻るときは再入国の手続きを確認する

有効なパスポートと在留カードを持つ人は、出国するときに再入国の意思を示せば、決められた期間内は再入国許可なしで戻れます（みなし再入国）。この期間は海外で延長できません。超える予定なら、出国前に再入国許可を取ります。期間は公式ページで確認してください。
