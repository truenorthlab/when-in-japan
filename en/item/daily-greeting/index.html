Everyday manners

Manners

Start with a light bow

A light nod or bow when you meet someone or enter or leave a shop comes across as polite. You do not need to memorize detailed rules of bowing. Exchanging greetings with your neighbors day to day makes trouble less likely.

Machine translation

Do

  • Give a light bow when you meet someone
  • Greet your neighbors when you see them

Avoid

  • Skip greetings because you worry about bowing correctly

Original Japanese

あいさつは軽いおじぎから

人に会ったときや店に出入りするときは、軽くうなずいたり頭を下げたりするだけで丁寧に伝わります。おじぎの細かい決まりを覚える必要はありません。近所の人と日ごろからあいさつを交わしておくと、トラブルが起きにくくなります。
