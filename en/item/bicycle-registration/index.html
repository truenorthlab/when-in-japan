Bicycles

Legal rule

Register your bicycle for crime prevention

By law, people who use a bicycle must have it registered for crime prevention (bohan toroku). This also applies to used or hand-me-down bicycles. Where to register depends on the area; in Tokyo, for example, it is done at authorized bicycle shops.

Machine translation

Do

  • Register at the shop when you buy a bicycle
  • For a bicycle you were given, check your area's procedure and register it

Avoid

  • Keep riding without registering

Official information

Original Japanese

自転車は防犯登録をする

自転車を利用する人は、防犯登録を受けることが法律で決められています。新品だけでなく、中古や人からもらった自転車も同じです。手続きの場所は地域によって異なり、例えば東京都では委託を受けた自転車販売店です。
