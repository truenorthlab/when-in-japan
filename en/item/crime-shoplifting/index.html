Crime & the law

Law

Shoplifting is a crime, even for small amounts

Taking goods from a shop without paying is shoplifting, which falls under the crime of theft. Small amounts are treated as the same crime. Returning the goods or paying for them afterwards does not settle the matter.

Machine translation

Do

  • Pay at the register before you leave the shop

Avoid

  • Take something because it is small or cheap
  • Think returning it will make it all right

Official information

Original Japanese

万引きは少額でも犯罪

店の商品を代金を払わずに持ち出す万引きは、窃盗罪にあたる犯罪です。金額が少なくても同じ犯罪として扱われます。商品を返したり弁償したりすれば済む問題ではありません。
