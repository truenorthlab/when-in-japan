Crime & the law

Law

Violence is a crime, even if no one is injured

Using violence against someone is the crime of assault even if they are not injured, and the crime of causing injury if they are. Even in a fight over drinks, hitting someone is a crime.

Machine translation

Do

  • If a fight seems likely, walk away
  • If you feel in danger, call 110

Avoid

  • Hit or kick anyone
  • Get physical because it is a drinking occasion

Original Japanese

暴力は、相手がけがをしなくても犯罪

人に暴力をふるうと、相手がけがをしなくても暴行の罪に、けがをさせれば傷害の罪になります。お酒の席のけんかでも、人を殴れば犯罪です。
