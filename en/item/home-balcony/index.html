Home & neighbors

Manners

Your balcony can be an escape route

Balconies can serve as escape routes in a fire or other emergency. Do not put large or heavy things in front of the partition next to your neighbor. Some buildings ban using fire, such as for barbecues, or hanging futons over the railing. The link is an example from UR rental housing. Check the rules of your building.

Machine translation

Do

  • Keep the escape path clear
  • Check the rules of your building

Avoid

  • Use fire when your building's rules ban it

Official information

Original Japanese

ベランダは避難路にもなる

ベランダやバルコニーは、火事などのときの避難路にもなります。隣との仕切り板の前に、大きな物や重い物を置かないようにします。バーベキューなどの火の使用や、手すりに布団を干すことを禁じている建物もあります。リンク先は UR 賃貸の例です。住んでいる建物の規約を確かめます。
