Local or facility ruleApplies in: Around Shibuya Station, Shibuya City, Tokyo

Don't drink on the street around Shibuya Station at night

Under a Shibuya City ordinance, drinking alcohol at night in public places such as streets and parks within a designated area around Shibuya Station is prohibited all year round. Check the area and hours on the map and guidance on the city's page.
