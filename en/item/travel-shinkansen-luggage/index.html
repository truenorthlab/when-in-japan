Reserve ahead for large luggage on the Shinkansen

On the Tokaido, Sanyo, Kyushu and Nishi-Kyushu Shinkansen, luggage over a certain size needs a reserved seat with a baggage space. Check the official guide for the size limits.

Machine translation

Do

  • Choose a "seat with oversized baggage area" when you buy your ticket
  • Consider a luggage courier or coin locker

Avoid

  • Board with large luggage without a reservation

Official information

Original Japanese

新幹線に大きな荷物を持ち込むときは予約する

東海道・山陽・九州・西九州新幹線では、一定より大きな荷物を持ち込むとき、荷物スペースつきの座席の予約が必要です。大きさの基準は公式案内で確認します。
