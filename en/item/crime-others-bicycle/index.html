Crime & the law

Law

Do not use someone else's bicycle without permission

Do not ride or take someone else's bicycle without permission. Even if a bicycle looks abandoned, taking it without permission is the crime of embezzling lost property.

Machine translation

Do

  • Ride your own bicycle or one you have rented or borrowed

Avoid

  • Ride someone else's bicycle on a whim
  • Take a bicycle that looks abandoned

Official information

Original Japanese

他人の自転車を勝手に使わない

他人の自転車に勝手に乗ったり、持っていったりしてはいけません。放置されているように見える自転車でも、勝手に持っていくと占有離脱物横領の罪になります。
