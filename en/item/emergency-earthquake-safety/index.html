Good to know

When the ground shakes, protect yourself first

When you see or hear an earthquake early warning, or feel shaking, stay calm and protect yourself first. Indoors, protect your head and take cover under a sturdy desk or similar. Do not rush outside, and do not force yourself to go and put out a fire. Outdoors, watch out for falling block walls, signs and broken glass. In an elevator, stop at the nearest floor and get out. On trains and buses, hold on firmly to a strap or handrail.

Machine translation

Do

  • Protect your head and take cover under a sturdy desk or similar
  • Outdoors, keep away from block walls, signs and glass
  • In an elevator, stop at the nearest floor and get out
  • On trains and buses, hold on firmly to a strap or handrail

Avoid

  • Rush outside in a panic
  • Force yourself to put out a fire while it is still shaking

  • Use the Disaster Message Dial 171 to reach familyIn a major disaster, phone lines get congested and the 171 service lets you leave voice messages. It isn't on in normal times, its voice guidance is in Japanese only, and it can't be used from overseas. web171 lets you leave text messages. Avoid phone calls where possible and use data.Good to know
  • Check evacuation sites on a hazard mapThe national Hazard Map Portal links to each municipality's hazard maps; the site is in Japanese only. Original Japanese

揺れたら、まず身の安全を守る

緊急地震速報を見聞きしたときや揺れを感じたときは、あわてずに、まず身の安全を守ります。家の中では頭を守り、丈夫な机の下などに隠れます。あわてて外へ飛び出したり、無理に火を消しに行ったりしません。外では、ブロック塀や看板、割れたガラスの落下に気をつけます。エレベーターでは最寄りの階で止めて降り、電車やバスではつり革や手すりをしっかり持ちます。
