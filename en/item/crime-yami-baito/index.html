Crime & the law

Legal rule

Do not answer 'easy, high-paying' criminal job ads

So-called yami-baito ads use words like 'high pay', 'same-day cash' or 'just collect some documents' to recruit people to carry out crimes. The ID you send when applying can be used to threaten you, with words like 'we will come to your home' or 'we will harm your family', and you may not be able to quit. If you are approached or have already applied, talk to the police right away.

Machine translation

Do

  • Talk to the police consultation line #9110 or your nearest police station
  • Check the work and the employer before you apply

Avoid

  • Send your ID without knowing what the work is
  • Follow instructions to install a highly anonymous app

「高収入・簡単」の闇バイトに応じない

「高額」「即日現金」「書類を受け取るだけ」といった言葉で、犯罪の実行役を集める「闇バイト」があります。応募のときに送った身分証明書を使って「家に行く」「家族に危害を加える」と脅され、やめられなくなることがあります。誘われたり応募してしまったりしたら、すぐ警察に相談してください。
