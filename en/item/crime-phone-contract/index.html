Crime & the law

Law

Do not hand over a contracted phone without permission

Handing a contracted mobile phone to someone without the phone company's consent can be punished as a violation of the law. Signing a contract with no intention of using it, then handing over the phone for money, is fraud against the shop. When you will stop using it, such as when you leave Japan, contact the phone company and cancel.

Machine translation

Do

  • Ask the phone company first if you want to pass a phone on
  • Contact the phone company and cancel before you leave Japan

Avoid

  • Accept offers of money for signing a phone contract
  • Give your ID documents to someone so they can sign a contract for you
