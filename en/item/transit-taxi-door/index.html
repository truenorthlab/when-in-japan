Trains & buses

Manners

Do not open or close taxi doors yourself

The rear left door of a taxi opens and closes automatically, and as a rule the rear right door is not opened or closed. You also do not need to tip.

Machine translation

Do

  • Wait for the door to open
  • Get in and out by the left door

Avoid

  • Open or close the left door by hand
  • Get in or out by the right door

Official information

Original Japanese

タクシーのドアは自分で開け閉めしない

タクシーの後部左側のドアは自動で開閉し、後部右側のドアは原則として開け閉めしません。チップを渡す必要もありません。
