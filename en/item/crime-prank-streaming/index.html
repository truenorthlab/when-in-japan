Crime & the law

Law

Don't disturb people or businesses for videos or live streams

Causing a disturbance or playing pranks in shops, on trains and so on to film or stream videos can be the crime of obstruction of business under the Penal Code if it interferes with people's work. Filming strangers without asking and streaming it may violate their portrait rights and privacy. When filming, follow the rules of the shop or facility and get permission from the people who appear.

Machine translation

Do

  • Ask for permission before filming in shops or facilities
  • Ask people who appear whether you may stream them

Avoid

  • Play pranks on shops, trains or passersby for views
  • Film strangers without asking and stream it
  • Film where filming is prohibited

Original Japanese

撮影や配信のために、人や店に迷惑をかけない

動画の撮影や配信のために、店や電車などで騒ぎを起こしたり、いたずらをしたりして仕事を妨げると、刑法の業務妨害罪になることがあります。知らない人を勝手に撮って配信すると、その人の肖像権やプライバシーを侵害するおそれがあります。撮影するときは店や施設のルールに従い、映る人の許可を取ります。
