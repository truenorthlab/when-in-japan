Do not rush onto trains as the doors close

Running onto a train as the doors are closing is dangerous: you can get caught in the doors or bump into people on the platform. Leave enough time to get to the station, and if you miss a train, wait for the next one.

Machine translation

Do

Original Japanese

駆け込み乗車をしない

閉まりかけたドアに走って乗り込むと、ドアに挟まれたり、ホームで人とぶつかったりして危険です。時間に余裕を持って駅に向かい、間に合わなければ次の電車を待ちます。
