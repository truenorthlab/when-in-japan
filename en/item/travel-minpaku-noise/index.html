Keep the noise down at night in vacation rentals

Vacation rentals (民泊) are often in residential areas where people live. By law, hosts must explain to guests what to keep in mind to avoid noise. Follow the rules you are given, and stay quiet at night and early in the morning.

Machine translation

Do

  • Follow the rules your host explains
  • Stay quiet at night and early in the morning

Avoid

  • Make noise in your room at night
  • Gather and make noise in front of the building or in the street

Official information

Original Japanese

民泊では夜の音に気をつける

民泊は住宅街にあることが多く、まわりで人が暮らしています。法律で、民泊の事業者は宿泊者に騒音を防ぐための注意を説明することになっています。説明された決まりを守り、夜や早朝は静かに過ごします。
