Good to know

Do not touch, talk to or feed assistance dogs at work

Assistance dogs, meaning guide dogs, service dogs and hearing dogs, are important partners of people with disabilities, not pets. Because they need to focus on their user's instructions, people are asked not to distract them by talking to them, staring at them or touching them. Do not give them food or water either. If the dog is distracted, its user can no longer walk safely.

Machine translation

Do

  • Know that assistance dogs may go with their users on transport and into shops
  • If the user seems to need help, speak to the user, not the dog, and ask if you can help

Avoid

  • Talk to, stare at or touch the dog
  • Give the dog food or water

Original Japanese

仕事中のほじょ犬に触ったり話しかけたり食べ物をあげたりしない

盲導犬、介助犬、聴導犬の「ほじょ犬」は、障害のある人の大切なパートナーで、ペットではありません。ほじょ犬はユーザーの指示に集中する必要があるため、話しかける、じっと見つめる、触るなど気を引く行為は避けるよう呼びかけられています。食べ物や水もあげません。気が散ると、ユーザーが安全に歩けなくなります。
