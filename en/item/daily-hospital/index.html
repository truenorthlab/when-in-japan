Good to know

For minor illness or injury, go to a nearby clinic

Medical institutions in Japan have different roles, and for minor illness or injury you first go to a nearby clinic. If you do not show your My Number health insurance card or similar proof, you pay the full medical costs yourself. If you cannot speak Japanese, you can ask your municipality, an international exchange association or an NPO for help.

Original Japanese

軽い病気やけがは近くの診療所へ

日本の医療機関は役割が分かれていて、軽い病気やけがなら、まず身近な診療所に行きます。マイナ保険証などを提示しないと、医療費が全額自己負担になります。日本語が話せないときは、市区町村や国際交流協会、NPO に相談できます。
