Trains & buses

Manners

Do not lie down on seats; take only one seat

Lying down on seats or stretching or spreading your legs keeps others from sitting and disturbs them. Sit close together, and keep your bags on your lap or on the luggage rack.

Machine translation

Do

  • Sit close together
  • Keep bags on your lap or on the luggage rack

Avoid

  • Lie down on the seats
  • Sit with your legs stretched out or spread wide
  • Put bags on the seats
  • Sit on the floor near the doors or in the aisle

Official information

Original Japanese

座席に寝そべらず、一人分に座る

座席に横になったり、足を伸ばしたり広げたりすると、ほかの人が座れず迷惑になります。座席は詰めて座り、荷物はひざの上や網棚に置きます。
