Garbage & recycling

Local or facility rule

Don't mix batteries such as power banks with other rubbish

If lithium-ion batteries, or products that use them such as power banks, get mixed in with other rubbish, they can cause fires in garbage trucks and waste facilities. When you throw them out from home, follow your municipality's rules for putting out rubbish.

Machine translation

Do

  • Check your municipality's rules for putting out rubbish
  • Ask your municipality if you are not sure how to put them out

Avoid

  • Put them out with burnable or non-burnable rubbish

Official information

Original Japanese

モバイルバッテリーなどの電池をほかのごみに混ぜない

リチウムイオン電池や、それを使ったモバイルバッテリーなどの製品がほかのごみに混ざると、ごみ収集車やごみ処理施設で火災の原因になります。家庭から出すときは、住んでいる市区町村のごみの出し方に従ってください。
