Residence status

Legal rule

Working on a student or similar status needs permission

People on a status with a limited scope of activity, such as student or dependent (Appended Table I of the Immigration Act), need permission to engage in activity outside that scope (shikakugai katsudo) to earn income. Adult entertainment businesses and similar are not eligible. Permanent residents, spouses of Japanese nationals, long-term residents and similar are not covered by this procedure.

Official information

Original Japanese

留学などの在留資格で働くには許可が必要

留学や家族滞在など、活動の範囲が決められた在留資格（入管法の別表第一）の人が、その範囲の外で収入を得る活動をするには、資格外活動許可が必要です。風俗営業などは許可の対象になりません。永住者、日本人の配偶者等、定住者などは、この手続きの対象ではありません。
