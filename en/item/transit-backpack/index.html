Trains & buses

Manners

Take off your backpack on crowded trains

On crowded trains, you are asked to hold backpacks and other bags in front of you, carry them by hand or put them on the overhead rack.

Machine translation

Do

  • Hold your backpack in front of you or carry it by hand
  • Use the overhead rack if you can

Avoid

  • Keep a backpack on your back in a crowded car

Official information

Original Japanese

混んだ車内ではリュックを背負わない

混雑した車内では、リュックなどの荷物は前に抱える、手に持つ、網棚に置くよう案内されています。
