Emergencies & disasters

Local or facility rule

Check evacuation sites on a hazard map

The national Hazard Map Portal links to each municipality's hazard maps; the site is in Japanese only. Designated emergency evacuation sites are where you flee when a disaster strikes, and designated shelters are where you stay for a while. They are different.

Machine translation

Do

  • Check the risks around your home, workplace and school
  • Check the evacuation site for each type of disaster

Avoid

  • Assume evacuation sites and shelters are the same

Original Japanese

ハザードマップで避難先を確認しておく

国のハザードマップポータルサイトから、市区町村のハザードマップを確認できます。サイトは日本語のみです。災害が起きたときに逃げるのは「指定緊急避難場所」、しばらく滞在するのは「指定避難所」で、別のものです。
