Home & neighbors

Manners

Check the rules before you get a pet

Most apartment buildings ban pets, and even where pets are allowed, the type, size and number are almost always set by the building's rules. Barking, smells and uncollected droppings are common causes of trouble with neighbors.

Machine translation

Do

  • Check your building rules and lease before getting a pet
  • Carry bags on walks and take droppings home

Avoid

  • Ignore the rules and keep a pet secretly
  • Leave droppings behind
