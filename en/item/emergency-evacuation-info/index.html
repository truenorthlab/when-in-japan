Emergencies & disasters

Local or facility rule

Evacuate early when an evacuation alert is issued

Evacuation information is issued by the mayor of the municipality. Be sure to evacuate by Alert Level 4, the evacuation order (hinan shiji). At Level 3, older people and others who need more time evacuate. Even with no alert, if you sense danger, decide for yourself and evacuate.

Machine translation

Do

  • Set up a way to receive your municipality's disaster information
  • If you sense danger, evacuate without waiting for an alert

Avoid

  • Wait for Level 5 (emergency safety measures) before moving

Official information

Original Japanese

避難情報が出たら早めに避難する

避難情報は市区町村長が出します。警戒レベル4の「避難指示」までに必ず避難します。レベル3の「高齢者等避難」では、高齢者など避難に時間がかかる人が避難します。情報が出ていなくても、危険を感じたら自分で判断して避難してください。
