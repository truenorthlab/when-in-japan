Smoking & drinking

Legal rule

No drinking under 20

The age of adulthood in Japan is 18, but the age limit for alcohol stays at 20. The law prohibits drinking under 20, and businesses may not sell or serve alcohol to anyone under 20.

Machine translation

Do

  • Don't drink until you are 20

Avoid

  • Offer alcohol to anyone under 20

Official information

Original Japanese

20歳未満はお酒を飲めない

日本の成年年齢は18歳ですが、お酒の年齢制限は20歳のままです。法律で20歳未満の飲酒は禁じられており、店が20歳未満の人にお酒を売ったり出したりすることも禁じられています。
