Trains & buses

Good to know

On buses, wait until the bus stops before you stand up

On local buses, passengers fall and get hurt when the bus shakes as it starts, stops or brakes suddenly. Many of those hurt are older people. Even as your stop gets close, stay seated until the bus has come to a complete stop. When standing, hold on firmly to a handrail or strap.

Machine translation

Do

  • Stand up only after the bus has come to a complete stop
  • Hold on firmly to a handrail or strap when standing

Avoid

  • Change seats or walk to the exit while the bus is moving

Original Japanese

バスでは、止まってから席を立つ

路線バスでは、発車や停車、急ブレーキのときの揺れで乗客が転び、けがをする事故が起きています。けがをする人には高齢の人が多くいます。降りる停留所が近づいても、バスが完全に止まってから席を立ちます。立っているときは、手すりやつり革をしっかり持ちます。
