Crime & the law

Legal rule

Sexual acts without consent are crimes

Engaging in a sexual act when the other person finds it difficult to form, express or maintain the will to refuse is punished as non-consensual indecency or non-consensual sexual intercourse. This includes situations such as being under the influence of alcohol or drugs, being asleep, being frozen with fear, or being unable to refuse because of a difference in position. It is punished whether or not the two people are married. Sexual acts with children under 16 are also punished (if the child is 13 or older, this applies to people who are at least five years older).
