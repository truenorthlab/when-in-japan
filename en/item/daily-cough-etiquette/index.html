Manners

Cover your mouth and nose when you cough or sneeze

When you cough or sneeze, cover your mouth and nose with a mask, tissue or handkerchief. If there is no time, use your sleeve or the inside of your elbow. Coughing or sneezing without regard for others is also listed among behaviors people find annoying on trains.

Machine translation

Do

  • Cover your mouth and nose with a mask, tissue or handkerchief
  • If there is no time, use your sleeve or the inside of your elbow

Avoid

  • Cough or sneeze openly toward people around you
