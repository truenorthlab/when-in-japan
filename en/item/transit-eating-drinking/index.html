Avoid eating and drinking on crowded trains

In a survey by a railway association, eating and drinking on crowded trains is listed among behaviors people find annoying. It is not a rule, but avoid it when the train is crowded.

Machine translation

Do

  • When it is crowded, eat and drink after you get off

Avoid

  • Eat or drink in a crowded car

混んだ車内では飲食を控える

鉄道会社の団体が行ったアンケートでは、混雑した車内での飲食が迷惑な行為の一つに挙げられています。決まりではありませんが、混んでいるときは控えます。
