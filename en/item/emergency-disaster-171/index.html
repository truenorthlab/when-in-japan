Emergencies & disasters

Good to know

Use the Disaster Message Dial 171 to reach family

In a major disaster, phone lines get congested and the 171 service lets you leave voice messages. It isn't on in normal times, its voice guidance is in Japanese only, and it can't be used from overseas. web171 lets you leave text messages. Avoid phone calls where possible and use data.

Machine translation

Do

  • Agree on how to reach your family in advance
  • If calls don't connect, use data or web171

Avoid

  • Assume 171 is always available

Official information

Original Japanese

災害時の連絡には災害用伝言ダイヤル171

大きな災害のときは電話がつながりにくくなり、音声で伝言を残せる「171」が運用されます。普段は使えず、音声案内は日本語のみで、海外からは使えません。文字で伝言を残せる「web171」もあります。電話はなるべく控え、データ通信を使いましょう。
