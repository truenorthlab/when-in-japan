Crime & the law

Legal rule

Do not sell or give away your bank account

Selling or handing over a bank account, cash card, passbook or login details is a crime. It can be a crime even if you receive no money. Close accounts you will no longer use, such as when you leave Japan, at your bank.

Machine translation

Do

  • Close your account at the bank before you leave Japan
  • If asked to sell your account, refuse and talk to the police

Avoid

  • Sell or give your account, card, passbook, PIN or login details to anyone
  • Buy or take over someone else's account

銀行口座を売らない・人に渡さない

銀行口座やキャッシュカード、通帳、ログイン情報を売ったり、人に渡したりすることは犯罪です。お金を受け取らない場合でも犯罪になることがあります。帰国などで使わなくなる口座は、金融機関で解約します。
