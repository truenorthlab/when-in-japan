Smoking & drinking

Legal rule

Never drive after drinking

The Road Traffic Act prohibits driving under the influence of alcohol, regardless of the amount. Not only the driver but also those who lend the car, serve the alcohol or ride along can be punished.

Machine translation

Do

  • Don't drink if you plan to drive
  • After drinking, use public transport or similar

Avoid

  • Drive because you think a little is fine
  • Ride in a car with a driver who has been drinking

Official information

Original Japanese

お酒を飲んだら運転しない

道路交通法は、酒気を帯びて車両等を運転することを、量にかかわらず禁じています。運転した人だけでなく、車を貸した人、お酒を出した人、同乗した人も処罰の対象になります。
