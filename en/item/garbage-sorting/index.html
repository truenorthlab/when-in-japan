Garbage & recycling

Local or facility rule

Sort garbage by your municipality's rules

Sorting categories and their names differ by area. What worked where you lived before may not apply after you move.

Machine translation

Do

  • Check your municipality's sorting chart
  • If unsure, ask the municipal office or check its website

Avoid

  • Keep using the rules of your previous area
