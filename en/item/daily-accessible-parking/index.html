Good to know

Keep accessible parking spaces free for the people who need them

The wide accessible parking spaces near the entrances of shops and facilities are for people who need extra consideration getting in and out of a car or moving around, such as wheelchair users, people with disabilities, older people and pregnant women. Wheelchair users in particular need to open the car door fully to get in and out, so they cannot use ordinary spaces. Even if one is empty, leave it free if you do not need it.

Machine translation

Do

  • Park in an ordinary space if you do not need an accessible one

Avoid

  • Park in an accessible space because it is empty or you will be quick

Related items

  • Drive on the left, and always stop at red lights and stop signsIn Japan, vehicles drive on the left side of the road. Stop at red lights, and where there is a stop sign (止まれ), come to a full stop before the stop line and check left and right. When someone is crossing or about to cross at a crosswalk, stop before it and let them go first. Traffic rules and signs in Japan can differ from those in other countries.Law
Original Japanese

障害者用駐車場は必要な人のために空けておく

店や施設の出入口の近くにある、幅の広い障害者用駐車場（障害者等用駐車区画）は、車椅子を使う人、障害のある人、高齢の人、妊産婦など、乗り降りや移動に配慮が必要な人のための区画です。特に車椅子を使う人は、車から乗り降りするのにドアを全開にする必要があり、普通の区画を使えません。空いていても、必要のない人は停めずに空けておきます。
