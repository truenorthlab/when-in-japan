Trains & buses

Law

Don't ride in unlicensed taxis (shirotaku) or carry paying passengers yourself

Carrying passengers for money in a private car without government permission is called "shirotaku" (白タク) and is prohibited under the Road Transport Act. You may be approached at airports or tourist spots, or such rides may be arranged through ride apps. In an unlicensed taxi, you may not receive proper compensation if there is an accident. Use licensed taxis or authorized services.

Machine translation

Do

  • Use licensed taxis at a taxi stand
  • With ride apps, check that the ride is a licensed taxi or an authorized service

Avoid

  • Get into a private car whose driver approached you at an airport or station
  • Carry people in your own car for money

Related items

  • Drive on the left, and always stop at red lights and stop signsIn Japan, vehicles drive on the left side of the road. Stop at red lights, and where there is a stop sign (止まれ), come to a full stop before the stop line and check left and right. When someone is crossing or about to cross at a crosswalk, stop before it and let them go first. Traffic rules and signs in Japan can differ from those in other countries.Law
  • Check your license is valid in Japan before drivingTo drive a car or motorcycle in Japan you need a Japanese license, an International Driving Permit under the treaty, or a foreign license with a designated translation. There is a limit on how long these can be used, and the eligible countries are set. Driving without a license is unlicensed driving.Law
