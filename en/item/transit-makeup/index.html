Don't do your makeup on the train

In a survey by an association of railway companies, putting on makeup on the train is listed as one of the behaviors people find annoying. Strong scents, such as from cosmetics, are also listed. It is not a rule, but do your makeup at home or in a restroom.

Machine translation

Do

  • Do your makeup at home or in a station restroom

Avoid

  • Put on makeup on a crowded train
Original Japanese

電車の中で化粧をしない

鉄道会社の団体が行ったアンケートでは、電車の中での化粧が、迷惑に感じる行為の一つに挙げられています。化粧品などの強い香りも挙げられています。決まりではありませんが、化粧は家や化粧室で済ませます。
