Smoking & drinking

Local or facility rule

Some areas ban smoking on the street

Some municipalities ban smoking on the street by ordinance and charge an administrative fine for violations. For example, the ban covers all of Chiyoda, Tokyo (except the Imperial Palace) and all of Osaka City. Zones and amounts differ by municipality.

Official information

Original Japanese

路上喫煙が禁止の地域がある

路上喫煙を条例で禁止し、違反すると過料を取る自治体があります。例えば東京都千代田区は皇居を除く全域、大阪市は市内全域が対象です。区域と金額は自治体ごとに違います。
