Crime & the law

Legal rule

Do not secretly film people's bodies or underwear

Secretly filming a person's sexual body parts or the underwear they are wearing, without a legitimate reason, is a crime. Giving the images to others or making them public is also punishable.

Machine translation

Do

  • If this happens to you, call 110 or the sexual crime victim line #8103

Avoid

  • Secretly film someone's body or underwear with a phone or other device
  • Send the images to others or post them online

Official information

Original Japanese

人の体や下着をこっそり撮影しない

正当な理由なく、人の性的な部位や身に着けている下着などをひそかに撮影することは犯罪です。撮影した画像を人に渡したり公開したりすることも処罰の対象です。
