Residence status

Legal rule

Pay your taxes and insurance, and ask early if you cannot

Resident tax applies regardless of nationality, and you also pay National Health Insurance premiums (or tax) and similar charges. When a foreign national applies to extend their period of stay or change their status of residence, their payment record may be checked, and unpaid amounts can affect the review. If you leave them unpaid, you may also face measures such as seizure of property. If paying is difficult, talk to your city office before it becomes overdue. The link is one ward's guide, so check how to pay and where to ask with the city office where you live.

Original Japanese

税金と保険料は納め、払えないときは早めに相談する

住民税は国籍にかかわらず課税され、国民健康保険料（税）なども納めます。外国籍の人が在留期間の更新や在留資格の変更を申請するとき、納付状況が確認されることがあり、未納があると審査に影響する場合があります。そのままにすると、財産の差押えなどの処分を受けることもあります。払うのが難しいときは、滞納になる前に市区町村の窓口に相談します。リンク先は一つの区の案内なので、納め方や相談先は住んでいる市区町村で確かめます。
