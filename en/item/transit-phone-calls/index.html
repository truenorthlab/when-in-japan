Trains & buses

Manners

Don't take phone calls on trains and buses

Passengers are asked to set phones to silent mode and refrain from calls. Near priority seats, some railways ask you to turn your phone off when the car is crowded.

Machine translation

Do

  • Set your phone to silent mode
  • Follow the onboard notices near priority seats

Avoid

  • Talk on the phone on board

Official information

Original Japanese

電車やバスの中では通話しない

車内ではスマートフォンをマナーモードにし、通話は控えるよう案内されています。優先席の近くでは、混雑時に電源を切るよう求める鉄道会社もあります。
