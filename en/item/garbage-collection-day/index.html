Garbage & recycling

Local or facility rule

Put garbage out on the set day, time and place

Each type of garbage has set collection days and hours. Hours vary by area: some ask you to put it out by morning, while others, such as Fukuoka City, collect at night.

Machine translation

Do

  • Check the collection days, hours and location in your municipality's guide or garbage calendar
  • Put garbage out at the set time, at the designated collection point

Avoid

  • Put garbage out on the wrong day or outside the set hours
