Crime & the law

Law

Groping is a serious crime

Groping people, such as on trains, is a serious crime that tramples on personal dignity. It is punished under local ordinances and the Penal Code. If this happens to you, do not hesitate to call 110.

Original Japanese

痴漢は重大な犯罪

電車の中などで人の体に触る痴漢は、個人の尊厳を踏みにじる重大な犯罪で、条例や刑法で処罰されます。被害にあったら、迷わず 110 番に通報してください。
