Moving

Manners

Arrange electricity, gas and water yourself

Before moving, tell the electricity company, the gas company and the municipal water office that you are moving. To start gas at your new home, you must be present when it is turned on.

Machine translation

Do

  • Contact each of them early once your move is set
  • Contact the gas company and be present when the gas is turned on

Avoid

  • Move out without telling them

電気・ガス・水道の手続きは自分でする

引っ越しの前に、電力会社、ガス会社、市区町村の水道担当へ移転の連絡をします。新しい家でガスを使い始めるときは、開栓に本人の立ち会いが必要です。
