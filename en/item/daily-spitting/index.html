Everyday manners

Legal rule

Do not spit or relieve yourself in the street

The Minor Offenses Act (軽犯罪法) prohibits spitting and urinating or defecating in streets, parks and other places where people gather. Violations can lead to short detention or a petty fine.

Machine translation

Do

  • Use the toilets at stations, parks and similar places
  • Spit into a tissue and throw it away

Avoid

  • Spit on the street or in a park
  • Relieve yourself on the street or behind something
