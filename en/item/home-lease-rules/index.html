Home & neighbors

Local or facility rule

No unapproved roommates or subletting

Letting someone live with you without notifying the landlord can get you asked to move out. Subletting a room requires the landlord's consent (Civil Code).

Machine translation

Do

  • Notify the landlord or agent when someone will live with you
  • Check the clauses on roommates and subletting in your contract

Avoid

  • Let family or friends move in without telling the landlord
  • Sublet the room without consent

Show in Japanese

無断で同居させない・又貸ししない

届け出のない人を無断で同居させると、退去を求められる場合があります。部屋を他の人に又貸しするには、貸主の承諾が必要です（民法）。
