Everyday life

Law

Follow the signals and use crosswalks

Pedestrians must also obey traffic signals. If there is a crosswalk nearby, cross there. Do not cross the road diagonally except where signs allow it, and do not cross right in front of or right behind a vehicle. Standing in the road in a way that blocks traffic, such as stopping in the roadway to take photos, is also prohibited by law.

Machine translation

Do

  • Follow the pedestrian signals
  • Cross at a nearby crosswalk
  • Take photos from a safe spot on the sidewalk

Avoid

  • Cross on a red light
  • Cross the road diagonally
  • Step into the roadway to take photos

Original Japanese

信号を守り、横断歩道を渡る

歩行者も信号に従わなければなりません。近くに横断歩道があるときはそこを渡り、標識で認められた場所を除いて道路を斜めに渡ったり、車のすぐ前やすぐ後ろを横切ったりしてはいけません。写真を撮るために車道で立ち止まるなど、交通の妨げになる方法で道路に立ち止まることも、法律で禁止されています。
