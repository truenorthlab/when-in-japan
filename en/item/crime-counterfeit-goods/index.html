Crime & the law

Law

Don't sell counterfeit brand goods or order them from abroad

Selling counterfeit goods made to look like a real brand infringes trademark rights and is punishable under the Trademark Act. Selling them on flea market apps or social media is the same. Counterfeit goods sent by businesses overseas are subject to confiscation at customs, even if they are for your own use. If an item is suspiciously cheap, check that it is genuine before buying.

Related items

  • Don't sell or give away medicine you brought from your home countrySelling or giving medicines to others without a license is prohibited under the Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices Act. Selling them on social media or flea market apps is the same. Even selling leftover medicine just once can be illegal. Medicines you brought in or ordered from abroad for your own use are only for you, and you may not sell or give them even to family or friends. If someone is unwell, suggest a clinic or pharmacy.Law
  • Don't resell concert or sports tickets above face valueUnder the Act on Ensuring Proper Distribution of Tickets for Entertainment Events, reselling qualifying concert, sports and other tickets above face value as a business, without the organizer's consent, is prohibited. Even a single resale can be judged as doing it as a business. Buying tickets in order to resell them is also prohibited. You may be refused entry with a resold ticket. If you can no longer go, use the official resale service approved by the organizer.Law
Original Japanese

偽ブランド品を売らない・取り寄せない

本物のブランドに似せた偽ブランド品を売ることは、商標権の侵害として商標法で処罰されます。フリマアプリやSNSで売ることも同じです。海外の事業者から送られてくる偽ブランド品は、自分で使うためでも、税関で没収の対象になります。安すぎる品物は、買う前に本物か確かめます。
