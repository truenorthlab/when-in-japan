Crime & the law

Legal rule

Do not carry blades

The law prohibits carrying blades without a legitimate reason such as work. Self-defense is not a legitimate reason. Even stationery such as scissors or box cutters can lead to police action if carried ready for use without a legitimate reason. Hiding a blade in a car can also count as carrying it.

Machine translation

Do

  • Take a blade you bought straight home
  • Carry work blades only when your work needs them

Avoid

  • Carry a blade for self-defense
  • Carry scissors or a box cutter ready for use

Show in Japanese

刃物を持ち歩かない

仕事などの正当な理由なく刃物を持ち歩くことは、法律で禁じられています。護身用は正当な理由にあたりません。はさみやカッターナイフなどの文房具でも、正当な理由なくすぐ使える状態で持ち歩くと取締りの対象になります。車の中に隠して持つことも、携帯にあたる場合があります。
