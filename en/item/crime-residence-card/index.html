Crime & the law

Legal rule

Do not give away your residence card or use someone else's

There are penalties for providing your own residence card to someone else, for using, receiving or holding a card in another person's name, and for involvement with forged cards. Foreign nationals who do these things, or who encourage or help others to do them, also fall under the grounds for deportation.

Machine translation

Do

  • Keep your residence card under your own control
  • If a request to hand over your card seems suspicious, contact an official service

Avoid

  • Give your card to someone else
  • Use, receive or hold a card in another person's name or a forged card
  • Ask for or help with these acts
