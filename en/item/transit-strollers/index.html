Trains & buses

Good to know

Be kind to people with strollers on trains and buses

The stroller mark shows places and equipment where people with strollers can feel safe, such as elevators and the spaces inside trains and buses. Places such as escalators have a no-stroller mark, meaning strollers must not be used there. People nearby are asked to be understanding, and to offer help where someone seems to be struggling, such as where there is no elevator. People with strollers also take care not to bump into others or block the way, and can ask for help without hesitation when they need it.

Machine translation

Do

  • Be understanding toward people with strollers
  • Offer help on stairs without an elevator if someone seems to be struggling
  • Use the places with the stroller mark when you have a stroller

Avoid

  • Use a stroller on an escalator
  • Block the aisle or doorway with a stroller

電車やバスのベビーカーを温かく見守る

ベビーカーマークは、エレベーターや電車・バスの車内スペースなど、ベビーカーを使う人が安心して使える場所や設備を示します。エスカレーターなどには、ベビーカーを使ってはいけないことを示すベビーカー使用禁止マークがあります。周りの人は温かく見守り、エレベーターのない場所などで困っていそうなら手伝いを申し出ます。ベビーカーを使う人も、周りの人にぶつけたり通路をふさいだりしないよう気をつけ、困ったときは遠慮せずに手助けを頼みます。
