In Biei, Hokkaido, don't walk into the fields on the hills

The beautiful scenery of Biei's hills is made up entirely of farmers' fields. The fields are the farmers' private property, and entering them without permission is illegal. Snow-covered hills are fields too, with wheat and other crops growing under the snow. Soil carried on your shoes can also harm the crops. Do not drive or walk onto gravel roads or farm roads either. Flying drones and aerial photography in the town are prohibited in principle.

Related items

  • Don't enter off-limits places, other people's fields or private roadsEntering a place where entry is prohibited, or someone else's fields, without a legitimate reason is prohibited under the Minor Offenses Act. Entering someone's home without permission can also be the crime of trespassing under the Penal Code. Some roads that anyone can walk on are private roads owned by individuals or others rather than the national or local government. Follow any signs closing the road or prohibiting photography. Even for photos or a shortcut, do not go in when there is a sign.Law
  • Check the flight rules before flying a droneFlying a drone in Japan may require registering the aircraft. Without permission, you cannot fly around airports, over densely populated districts and in similar areas.Law

Original Japanese

北海道・美瑛の丘では畑に入らない

美瑛の丘の美しい景色は、すべて農家の畑です。畑は農家の私有地で、勝手に入ることは違法です。雪に覆われた丘も畑で、雪の下では小麦などの作物が育っています。靴についた土を通して、作物に害を与えるおそれもあります。砂利道や農道にも入りません。町の中でドローンを飛ばすことや空からの撮影は、原則として禁止されています。
