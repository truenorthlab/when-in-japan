Garbage & recycling

Local or facility rule

Apply in advance for oversized garbage

Large items such as furniture may not go out as regular garbage. In Shinjuku and Nakano (Tokyo) and Fukuoka City, for example, you must apply in advance, and Shinjuku charges a fee. Check your municipality's rules.

Machine translation

Do

  • Check which items count and how to apply in your municipality's guide
  • Check whether there is a fee and how to pay it

Avoid

  • Leave items at the collection point without applying

Official information

Original Japanese

粗大ごみは事前に申し込む

大きな家具などは、普通のごみとして出せないことがあります。例えば東京都新宿区・中野区や福岡市では事前の申し込みが必要で、新宿区では有料です。決まりは市区町村で確認してください。
