Moving

Legal rule

Submit a moving-out notification before you move

When moving to another municipality, submit a moving-out notification (tenshutsu-todoke) to your current municipality in advance. This generally also applies when leaving Japan to live abroad.

Machine translation

Do

  • Complete it at the municipal counter before you move
  • With a My Number Card, you can submit it online through Mynaportal

Avoid

  • Move without notifying

Official information

Original Japanese

引っ越す前に転出届を出す

別の市区町村へ引っ越すときは、前もって今の市区町村に転出届を出します。日本を出て海外で暮らす場合も、原則として届け出が必要です。
