Travel & sightseeing

Legal rule

Check the rules before bringing medicine

Some medicines brought from abroad have quantity limits or need procedures in advance. Amphetamines and cannabis cannot be brought in, even for medical treatment.

Machine translation

Do

  • Check the official guidance for your type of medicine before you leave
  • Keep a copy of your prescription or a doctor's certificate with prescribed medicine

Avoid

  • Bring medicine without checking whether a procedure is needed
  • Carry medicine for someone else

薬を持ち込む前にルールを確認する

海外から持ち込む薬には、数量の決まりや事前の手続きが必要なものがあります。アンフェタミンなどの覚醒剤や大麻は、治療のためでも持ち込めません。
