Everyday manners

Manners

Mind your voice and how much space you take up in crowded places

Talking loudly in crowded places such as streets and station passages, or blocking the way in a large group, is seen as a nuisance. Talking itself is not the problem, and a soft voice is fine.

Machine translation

Do

  • Lower your voice in crowded places
  • Keep aisles and doorways clear

Avoid

  • Block the way by spreading out in a large group
  • Stop in front of doorways

Official information

Original Japanese

人の多い場所では声と広がり方に気をつける

道や駅の通路など人の多い場所で大声で話したり、大勢で道をふさいだりすると、迷惑な行為と受け取られます。話すこと自体がいけないわけではなく、小さな声なら問題ありません。
