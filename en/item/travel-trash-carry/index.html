Travel & sightseeing

Good to know

Carry your trash if there is no bin

Public trash cans are limited in Japan. Sort your trash if you find a bin. If you don't, carry it with you and dispose of it later.

Machine translation

Do

  • Carry a small bag for trash
  • Put cans, glass and plastic bottles in the right bins

Avoid

  • Leave trash on the street or at sightseeing spots

Original Japanese

ごみ箱がなければ持ち帰る

町なかのごみ箱は多くありません。ごみ箱があれば分けて捨て、なければ持ち歩いて後で捨てます。
