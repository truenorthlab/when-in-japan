Public baths & hot springs

  • No photos in changing rooms or bathsTaking photos in changing rooms and bathing areas is prohibited by facility rules. In addition, secretly photographing someone's naked body or other sexual images can be punished by law.Legal rule
  • Tattoo policies differ by facilityHow people with tattoos are treated varies widely by facility, and you may be refused entry to the bath. Some places suggest covering with stickers, coming at a different time or using a private bath.Local or facility rule
  • Dry off before returning to the changing roomWhen going back from the bathing area to the changing room, dry your body well with a towel.Manners
  • Keep your towel out of the bath waterTo keep the water clean, towels should not touch the water in the tub.Manners
  • Wash before getting in the bathAt public baths and hot springs, wash your body first to keep the shared tub clean.Manners
