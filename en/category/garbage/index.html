Garbage & recycling

  • Recycle air conditioners, TVs, fridges and washersAir conditioners, TVs, refrigerators and freezers, and washing machines and clothes dryers fall under the Home Appliance Recycling Act. Hand them to an appliance retailer or follow your municipality's instructions. The consumer pays the recycling and collection fees.Legal rule
  • Do not litter or dump garbage illegallyThe Waste Management Act says no one may improperly discard waste, and violations carry penalties. Leaving garbage on a road or in the mountains is one example. Carelessly throwing cans, cigarette butts and other litter on streets or in parks is also prohibited by the Minor Offenses Act (軽犯罪法).Legal rule
  • Put garbage out on the set day, time and placeEach type of garbage has set collection days and hours. Hours vary by area: some ask you to put it out by morning, while others, such as Fukuoka City, collect at night.Local or facility rule
  • Apply in advance for oversized garbageLarge items such as furniture may not go out as regular garbage. In Shinjuku and Nakano (Tokyo) and Fukuoka City, for example, you must apply in advance, and Shinjuku charges a fee. Check your municipality's rules.Local or facility rule
  • Sort garbage by your municipality's rulesSorting categories and their names differ by area. What worked where you lived before may not apply after you move.Local or facility rule
