Moving

  • Notify your municipality after movingAfter you start living at a new address, submit a moving-in notification (tennyu-todoke) to the municipality within the legal deadline. Moves within the same municipality use a change-of-address notification (tenkyo-todoke). Failing to notify without good reason can lead to an administrative fine. Check the deadline on the official page.Law
  • Submit a moving-out notification before you moveWhen moving to another municipality, submit a moving-out notification (tenshutsu-todoke) to your current municipality in advance. This generally also applies when leaving Japan to live abroad.Law
  • It's customary to greet neighbors after movingIt isn't required, but people often greet their neighbors after moving in: the rooms on either side in an apartment building, or a few nearby houses. This follows a guide from Higashihiroshima City, Hiroshima.Good to know
  • Leave nothing behind when you move outWhen you leave a room, leave neither belongings nor garbage. This is a promise on the checklist in the housing guidebook from the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism.Good to know
  • Arrange electricity, gas and water yourselfBefore moving, tell the electricity company, the gas company and the municipal water office that you are moving. To start gas at your new home, you must be present when it is turned on.Good to know
