Travel & sightseeing

  • Check the flight rules before flying a droneFlying a drone in Japan may require registering the aircraft. Without permission, you cannot fly around airports, over densely populated districts and in similar areas.Legal rule
  • Check the rules before bringing medicineSome medicines brought from abroad have quantity limits or need procedures in advance. Amphetamines and cannabis cannot be brought in, even for medical treatment.Legal rule
  • Carry your passport while travelingVisitors without a residence card must carry their passport at all times and show it when a police officer or other authorized official asks. There are exceptions, so check the official text for details.Legal rule
  • Reserve ahead for large luggage on the ShinkansenOn the Tokaido, Sanyo, Kyushu and Nishi-Kyushu Shinkansen, luggage over a certain size needs a reserved seat with a baggage space. Check the official guide for the size limits.Local or facility rule
  • Don't eat while walking in crowded placesEating while walking is seen as impolite in most public places. In narrow or crowded streets, you may also soil other people's clothes.Manners
  • Keep the noise down at night in vacation rentalsVacation rentals (民泊) are often in residential areas where people live. By law, hosts must explain to guests what to keep in mind to avoid noise. Follow the rules you are given, and stay quiet at night and early in the morning.Manners
  • Be considerate of people and places in photosAsk before photographing people you don't know. Don't enter private land or go past fences for a photo, and follow no-photography signs.Manners
  • Be quiet and respectful at shrines and templesShrines and temples are places of worship. Behave quietly and follow the signs and instructions. If you are unsure how to pray, simply following those around you is fine.Manners
  • Carry your trash if there is no binPublic trash cans are limited in Japan. Sort your trash if you find a bin. If you don't, carry it with you and dispose of it later.Manners
  • Wear a yukata with the left side over the rightRyokan and hot spring inns often provide a yukata in your room. Fold the left side of the yukata over the right side, and tie the obi belt at the front of your waist.Manners
