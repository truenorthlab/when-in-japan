Residence status

  • Report your new address to the municipality (residence card)Mid- to long-term residents who move to a new home must report it at the municipal counter, not the immigration office, within the deadline. You must show your residence card. Failing to report carries penalties and can be grounds for revoking your status of residence. Check the deadline on the official page.Law
  • Always carry your residence cardMid- to long-term residents must always carry their residence card and show it when asked. Those under 16 are exempt from carrying it. Special permanent residents need not always carry their certificate but must show it when asked. Short-term visitors without a residence card carry their passport.Law
  • Check re-entry rules before leaving JapanPeople with a valid passport and residence card can come back without a re-entry permit within a set period if they say they intend to return when leaving (special re-entry). This period can't be extended abroad. If you expect to go beyond it, get a re-entry permit before you leave. Check the period on the official page.Law
  • Apply to renew before your period of stay endsTo keep staying, apply for renewal before your period of stay expires at the regional immigration office covering where you live. People 18 or older apply in person unless using an authorized agent. Check when you can apply and the fee on the official page.Law
  • Pay your taxes and insurance, and ask early if you cannotResident tax applies regardless of nationality, and you also pay National Health Insurance premiums (or tax) and similar charges. When a foreign national applies to extend their period of stay or change their status of residence, their payment record may be checked, and unpaid amounts can affect the review. If you leave them unpaid, you may also face measures such as seizure of property. If paying is difficult, talk to your city office before it becomes overdue. The link is one ward's guide, so check how to pay and where to ask with the city office where you live.Law
  • Working on a student or similar status needs permissionPeople on a status with a limited scope of activity, such as student or dependent (Appended Table I of the Immigration Act), need permission to engage in activity outside that scope (shikakugai katsudo) to earn income. Adult entertainment businesses and similar are not eligible. Permanent residents, spouses of Japanese nationals, long-term residents and similar are not covered by this procedure.Law
