About this app

Labels show whether something is a manner, a local rule or a legal rule. Every item has official sources and the date it was checked.

FAQ

Do I need an account?
No. Both the iOS app and this website are free and need no account.
Does it work without internet?
The iOS app keeps its content on your device, so you can read it offline. This website needs a connection.
Can I ask about my own situation?
No. We provide general information and do not assess personal situations. Please use the official help desks.
Is my location sent anywhere?
No. "Find from my location" in the iOS app works out your prefecture on your device and does not send your location anywhere.
