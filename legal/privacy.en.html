Privacy Policy

This policy explains how TrueNorthLabs Inc. ("we" or "us") handles information in the app "When in Japan" ("App") and its website.

This English version is a translation for reference. The Japanese version is the original and prevails if the two differ.

1. Information we do not collect

The App has no accounts or logins, no analytics tools and no advertising. We do not collect your name, email address, location, device identifiers or usage history through the App.

2. Information kept only on your device

The following is stored only on your device and is never sent to us. It is removed when you delete the App.

  • The language you chose
  • The items you saved
  • The area you chose
  • Your agreement to the disclaimer and the time content updates were last checked
  • The date you first read an item, the number of items you have read and the last time we asked for a rating, used to decide when to ask (which items you read is not stored)

"Find from my location" asks for location permission, then uses your current location once, on your device, to find your prefecture. Your location is not saved and is not sent to us or anyone else. Only the prefecture you confirm is saved.

The app sometimes shows Apple's rating dialog. Ratings and reviews go directly to Apple, and the app does not receive them.

3. Content updates

To keep its content current, the App requests content from a delivery server about once a day. We use GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (USA), for delivery. Because of how the internet works, your IP address and the versions of the App and your device's operating system reach the server. GitHub, Inc. logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. We do not receive these logs and do not use this information to identify you. For how GitHub, Inc. handles information, see its privacy statement.

4. Website

The website (truenorthlab.github.io/when-in-japan) has no accounts, cookies, analytics tools or advertising. Search words are handled inside your browser and are not sent to the server. The website is delivered by the same service as the App, so your IP address and browser information reach the server. We do not use this information to identify you.

5. Links to other sites

Opening a source or official link takes you to an external site. That site's own policy applies there.

The developer website link in Settings takes you to our company website. The address includes a tag showing you came from this app, but nothing that identifies you. Our company website uses an analytics tool, and its own privacy policy applies there.

6. Reporting errors

Tapping "Report an error" opens a draft in your mail app. You decide whether to send it. The item ID, content and App versions, and language are filled in. We use any email you send only to review your report and reply.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will announce it in the App.
